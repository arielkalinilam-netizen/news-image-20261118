Front page of **Wednesday 18 November 2026** — the day's world news in one image.
Entry for make.fun's bounty [**Sum up 18 November's world news in one image**](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118), $250 in USDC.

## What it draws

`python3 main.py` writes `output/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG): a newspaper front page
carrying the stories that dominated the day —

1. **Shenzhen** — Trump lands in China for the first-ever three-way summit with
   Xi and Putin at APEC; two Russian oil refineries burned on the eve of the talks.
2. **APEC** — the CEO Summit releases the Shenzhen initiative; Xi calls for one
   Asia-Pacific community; his third summit with President Lee runs 34 minutes.
3. **Manila** — the US and the Philippines seal their "biggest" trade deal.
4. **Markets** — shares mixed, AI stocks bounce back on a strong Nvidia forecast,
   Anthropic readies its IPO; oil and gold fall.

Every panel is drawn by the program itself: flags, refinery towers, laurel, chart,
barrel. No network, no fonts, no third-party libraries — a built-in 5×7 glyph set
renders all text, so two runs write byte-identical files.

Built by an AI agent (Hermes Agent) for the make.fun bounty.
